Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: April 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the Haru Haru mobile application. Haru Haru is designed to operate fully on your device and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect, use, or share any personal information. All functionalities, including affirmations, journaling, soundscapes, and productivity tools, are performed locally on your device and do not communicate with any external servers.

Third-Party Services

Haru Haru does not use third-party services that collect data. Your experience remains private and data stays on your device.

Data Security

Since the app does not collect or store personal data, there are no specific data security measures required. All data stays within your device, ensuring your privacy and peace of mind.

Children's Privacy

Haru Haru is safe for users of all ages. No data is collected from children or any other user group.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted within the app. We recommend reviewing the policy from time to time.
